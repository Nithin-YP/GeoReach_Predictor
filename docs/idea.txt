🛰️ Project Title: GeoReach Predictor
🎯 Elevator Pitch

GeoReach Predictor is a Django-based interactive GIS platform that helps entrepreneurs visualize, simulate, and predict the market reach and success probability of a new product before launch.
By adjusting parameters such as product type, luxury level, price, and target audience, users instantly see geographic clusters of potential customers and how their market reach could grow or shrink over time.

💡 Core Idea

Before launching a product, an entrepreneur often asks:

“Where should I target my launch, and what price will maximize reach?”

GeoReach Predictor answers this by combining demographic, expenditure, interest, and employment data with predictive analytics to simulate market success across geographic clusters.

🧭 Core Features
Feature	Description
Dynamic Parameter Adjustment	User adjusts product type, luxury level, target audience, price, and area/state on the dashboard.
GIS-Based Visualization	Interactive map (using Leaflet.js or Django Leaflet) highlights clusters of potential customers within the selected area.
Cluster Categorization	Color-coded clusters:
🟢 High potential (early adopters)
🟡 Medium potential (secondary)
🔴 Low potential (weak interest)
Time Projection Animation	Simulates potential market growth or shrinkage over the next few months based on demographic and economic trends.
Reach & Success Score	Outputs two numbers — Predicted Reach (% of target audience likely to convert) and Success Probability (likelihood of product-market fit).
Competitive Price Simulation (optional)	User can input a competitor’s price to see relative advantage or disadvantage in the same region.

🧠 Machine Learning Model
Goal:

Predict market reach and success probability for a product based on region-specific demographics, expenditure, and interests.

Model Flow:

Input Features

Region (state, city, coordinates)

Demographics (age, income, employment, population density)

Product attributes (luxury level, category, price)

Interest indicators (from dataset clusters)

Target Variables

Adoption Rate (%) or “Success Likelihood”

Model Choice

For quick hackathon use:
→ Random Forest Regressor or XGBoost Regressor (for prediction)
→ K-Means Clustering (for GIS segmentation)

Output

Cluster labels (for visualization colors)

Predicted reach (%)

Predicted growth rate over time (% increase/decrease)

Sample ML Pipeline:
from sklearn.ensemble import RandomForestRegressor
from sklearn.cluster import KMeans
from sklearn.preprocessing import StandardScaler
import pandas as pd

# Preprocess
data = pd.read_csv('dataset.csv')
X = data[['income', 'population_density', 'avg_spend', 'interest_score', 'price']]
y = data['adoption_rate']

# Scaling + Model
scaler = StandardScaler()
X_scaled = scaler.fit_transform(X)

model = RandomForestRegressor()
model.fit(X_scaled, y)

# Clustering for Map Visualization
kmeans = KMeans(n_clusters=3)
clusters = kmeans.fit_predict(X_scaled)

data['cluster'] = clusters
