Project Statement
GeoReach Predictor – v1.0
________________


Why is this project needed and relevant
Entrepreneurs often face uncertainty when deciding where, how, and at what price to launch a new product. Traditional market research is time-consuming and expensive, making it inaccessible for startups or small businesses.
GeoReach Predictor solves this by using data-driven insights to predict market reach, visualize customer clusters on a map, and estimate product success probability — all interactively and instantly.
This tool bridges geospatial intelligence, consumer analytics, and predictive modeling to help validate product-market fit before launch, making it highly relevant in today’s data-centric entrepreneurship ecosystem.
________________


Requirements to build
You are required to build an interactive web-based predictive analytics platform that allows entrepreneurs to:
1. Input parameters such as product type, luxury level, price, and target audience.

2. Select a geographic region (e.g., a state or city).

3. Visualize color-coded clusters of potential customers on a map interface.

4. See projections of how market potential could grow or shrink over time.

5. Receive predictive outputs:

   * Predicted Market Reach (%)

   * Product Success Probability (0–1)

The platform will use an ML model (RandomForest + KMeans) trained on demographic and expenditure datasets to simulate adoption rates and visualize consumer segmentation.
________________

Roles & Functionalities
This platform has 2 roles:
1. Admin (pre-user/superuser)
 2. Entrepreneur (primary user)
________________


Admin (pre-user/superuser)
Admin is the pre-existing superuser of the application.
Functionalities:
                              1. CRUD of Other Roles

                                 * Create, read, update, and delete Entrepreneur accounts.

                                 * Reset passwords or deactivate users if needed.

                                    2. Dataset Management

                                       * Upload and update core datasets (demographic, expenditure, geographic).

                                       * Validate dataset integrity before retraining the ML model.

                                          3. Model Retraining Trigger

                                             * Run batch jobs (via Django management command) to retrain or refresh the predictive model on updated datasets.

                                                4. Monitor System Activity

                                                   * View logs of all user predictions, areas queried, and model usage statistics.

                                                   * Check API performance or identify failed prediction calls.

                                                      5. Search and Filter Roles

                                                         * Search for users by username, region, or last login.

                                                         * Filter based on active/inactive status.

                                                            6. View Global Analytics Dashboard

                                                               * Aggregate summary: total predictions made, most queried states, and average predicted reach score across users.

________________


Entrepreneur (primary user)
The main user who interacts with the platform to evaluate potential market success for their product idea.
Functionalities:
                                                                  1. Secure Login & Authentication

                                                                     * Access via credentials assigned by admin.

                                                                        2. Input Product Parameters

                                                                           * Select:

                                                                              * Product Type (e.g., Apparel, Tech Device, Service)

                                                                              * Product Luxury Level (e.g., Basic, Mid-range, Premium)

                                                                              * Target Audience (e.g., Students, Professionals, Families)

                                                                              * Price Range (adjustable slider)

                                                                              * Geographic Area (choose state or district)

                                                                                 3. Trigger Prediction

                                                                                    * Sends input parameters to the backend API.

                                                                                    * ML model returns:

                                                                                       * Predicted Market Reach (% of target audience)

                                                                                       * Product Success Probability (0–1 score)

                                                                                          4. Interactive GIS Visualization

                                                                                             * Map (via Leaflet.js) highlights clusters:

                                                                                                * 🟢 High Potential

                                                                                                * 🟡 Medium Potential

                                                                                                * 🔴 Low Potential

                                                                                                   * Hovering on clusters reveals demographics like income, spending power, and employment rate.

                                                                                                      5. Time-Based Growth Projection

                                                                                                         * Chart.js graph showing how potential adoption might evolve over 6–12 months.

                                                                                                            6. Result Dashboard View

                                                                                                               * Displays a concise summary:

                                                                                                                  * Predicted Reach: 72%

                                                                                                                  * Success Probability: 0.81

                                                                                                                  * Top Clusters: Delhi Urban, Bengaluru Tech Corridor

                                                                                                                     7. Export/Download Results (Optional)

                                                                                                                        * Export the prediction report as CSV or PDF for investor or marketing reference.

                                                                                                                           8. View Past Predictions

                                                                                                                              * Access previous analyses stored in database (linked to user session).

                                                                                                                              * Re-run or compare predictions for different configurations.
















Key Terminologies
Admin (superuser)
A user with the highest level of access who manages roles, datasets, and overall system functionality.
                                                                                                                                 * Has authority to create/delete users, upload datasets, retrain ML models, and monitor analytics.

________________


Entrepreneur (primary user)
The main role of the platform.
                                                                                                                                    * Represents an entrepreneur or startup founder who wants to test the market viability of a product before launching it.

                                                                                                                                    * Uses predictive analytics and GIS visualization to identify where the product would gain traction and how price impacts adoption.

________________


Action/Function: Prediction Request
Description:
 A process initiated when an entrepreneur submits product and market parameters for analysis.
The backend model processes input data and returns predicted reach, success probability, and cluster segmentation.
Attributes:
                                                                                                                                       * Product Type

                                                                                                                                       * Product Luxury Level

                                                                                                                                       * Target Audience Category

                                                                                                                                       * Price Range

                                                                                                                                       * Geographic Area (State/City)

                                                                                                                                       * DateTime of Request

                                                                                                                                       * Result ID (linked to prediction result)

________________


Action/Function: Prediction Result
Description:
 The output generated by the ML engine based on the entrepreneur’s input.
Displays on a dashboard with numerical results, charts, and map highlights.
Attributes:
                                                                                                                                          * Predicted Market Reach (%)

                                                                                                                                          * Product Success Probability (0–1 scale)

                                                                                                                                          * High/Medium/Low Potential Cluster Count

                                                                                                                                          * Dominant Region (Top Cluster by Probability)

                                                                                                                                          * Growth Projection (time-series % increase/decrease)

                                                                                                                                          * Model Confidence Score

________________


Action/Function: Cluster Visualization
Description:
 A GIS-based map highlighting audience clusters by potential level.
High, Medium, and Low clusters are color-coded for easy interpretation.
Attributes:
                                                                                                                                             * Cluster ID

                                                                                                                                             * Latitude

                                                                                                                                             * Longitude

                                                                                                                                             * Cluster Label (High/Medium/Low)

                                                                                                                                             * Population Density

                                                                                                                                             * Average Income

                                                                                                                                             * Employment Rate

                                                                                                                                             * Interest Index

                                                                                                                                             * Region Name

________________


Action/Function: Dataset Management (Admin)
Description:
 Admin uploads and manages demographic and expenditure datasets used for predictions.
These datasets form the foundation of the ML model.
Attributes:
                                                                                                                                                * Dataset Name

                                                                                                                                                * Source (e.g., Kaggle, Census, Custom CSV)

                                                                                                                                                * Upload Date

                                                                                                                                                * Last Updated

                                                                                                                                                * Record Count

                                                                                                                                                * Columns/Features Used (Income, Population, Expenditure, etc.)

________________


Action/Function: Model Retraining (Admin)
Description:
 An administrative action to retrain the ML model using new or updated datasets to improve accuracy.
Attributes:
                                                                                                                                                   * Retrain Job ID

                                                                                                                                                   * Dataset Version Used

                                                                                                                                                   * Model Version

                                                                                                                                                   * Training Timestamp

                                                                                                                                                   * Model Accuracy (Validation Score)

                                                                                                                                                   * Updated By (Admin Username)

________________


Action/Function: User Management (Admin)
Description:
 Allows the admin to create, update, or deactivate entrepreneur accounts and manage permissions.
Attributes:
                                                                                                                                                      * User ID

                                                                                                                                                      * Username

                                                                                                                                                      * Role Type

                                                                                                                                                      * Account Status (Active/Inactive)

                                                                                                                                                      * Date Created

                                                                                                                                                      * Last Login

________________


Action/Function: Analytics Dashboard (Admin)
Description:
 Displays an overview of overall platform usage and system insights.
Attributes:
                                                                                                                                                         * Total Users

                                                                                                                                                         * Total Predictions Made

                                                                                                                                                         * Most Queried Regions

                                                                                                                                                         * Average Success Probability

                                                                                                                                                         * Most Common Product Category

                                                                                                                                                         * Model Accuracy Trend
